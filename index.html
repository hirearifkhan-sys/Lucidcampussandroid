Daily run rate explained

Formula
Remaining work ÷ study days

Revision buffer is removed from the available calendar days. Weekly study days are then used to estimate how many actual study sessions remain.

A calculated target is a planning aid, not a guarantee. Some topics take longer than others; update your totals and progress regularly.
